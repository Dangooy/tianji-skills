# Roadmap

Tianji Skills grows from repeated, real manufacturing-export work—not from a target count of skills.

## Near-term focus

- Improve installation and first-run guidance for the stable skills.
- Publish fully fictional or de-identified examples that demonstrate inputs, review gates, and expected outputs.
- Strengthen feedback collection around installation friction, industry adaptation, and failure cases.
- Keep skills usable across agent environments where their required tools and permissions are available.

## Inclusion standard

A skill belongs here only when it solves a recurring business problem, has explicit inputs and outputs, makes uncertainty visible, and can be demonstrated without exposing operational data.

See [PUBLICATION_POLICY.md](PUBLICATION_POLICY.md) for the public/private boundary.
